# Real NSW Selective Calibration Patterns (2021–2023)

Craft and distributional patterns from real 2021–2023 NSW SHSPT papers (Cambridge/NSW DoE). Use purely for structural fidelity and reasoning authenticity — never copy real passages, scenarios, or wording. Locked 2025+ computer-based exam counts/timing live in `SKILL.md` (do NOT web-search NSW/NESA).

## 1. Reading Craft Patterns
- **Part 1 (`READ_FICTION_MCQ`, 8 Qs):** Alternate single literary extract (~550–700w; Q1–6 paragraph-anchored inference/tone/idiom, Q7–8 whole-text arc) with **paired extracts** (`Text A` fiction + `Text B` non-fiction, `genreOrTextType: "paired_extracts"`, `extracts: [A, B]`) featuring cross-text qualification/synthesis Qs.
- **Part 2 (`READ_VOCAB_CLOZE`, 8 gaps):** ~180–300w informational passage; 2–3 grammatical options per gap so exact meaning, collocation, and discourse connectors decide.
- **Part 3 (`READ_POETRY_MCQ`, 6 Qs):** Full 20–28 line poem progressing from literal sense/narrator → figurative imagery → structural/repetition effect → symbolic/tonal shift.
- **Part 4 (`READ_GAPPED_TEXT`, 6 gaps, A–G w/ 1 decoy):** Alternate removed sentences vs paragraph summary headings (`{{gap:1}}`–`{{gap:6}}` above numbered paragraphs) with a keyword-sharing decoy.
- **Part 5 (`READ_MULTIPLE_MATCH`, 10 stmts, A–D):** Four voices (~140–180w each) sharing overlapping surface details so strict qualifiers decide.
- **Distractors:** Plausible motives, reversed cause/effect, half-true wrong-paragraph details, or literal readings of figurative language.

## 2. Mathematical Reasoning Craft Mechanisms (Rotate Broadly)
Problem-solving first, clean no-calculator arithmetic, 55–65% visuals, `[diagram not to scale]` on schematic figures, and 2–3 multi-statement (`1/2/3`) items per paper:
1. **Parity, remainders & number properties:** Remainder classes (`mod n`), parity from symbol equations, LCM pyramids, LCM exclusion ("A and B flash together *without* C").
2. **Missing-digit & bounds:** Maximising/minimising with unknown digits (`$70.2■` vs `$68.5▲`), digit counts of products, digit cards placed to fix median.
3. **3D spatial & scaling:** Painted faces/edges of `n×n×n` cube stacks, adding/removing cubes, patterned cube/prism nets, 2D shadow projections of 3D solids, volume/mass scaling (`2×` length → `8×` volume).
4. **2D geometry, symmetry & motion:** Overlapping rectangles with shaded voids, consecutive reflections tracking labelled corners, compass polygon walks, minimum extra squares for symmetry, map scale area (`1:k` → `k²`).
5. **Data & incomplete statistics:** Bar graphs with missing bars + ratio clues, incomplete frequency tables with mode constraints, total count from frequency charts (`Σ value × frequency`, catching sum-of-bars trap).
6. **Hidden-composition probability:** Deduce unknown coin/marble counts from total value + one probability before finding a second probability.
7. **Time, cycles & units:** Cross-date time zones, faulty clocks gaining/losing min/hr, repeating shape cycles (`92nd`/`93rd`), spiral coordinate walks, unfamiliar chained unit conversions.

## 3. Thinking Skills Blueprint (40 Qs)
Split ~40–45% Verbal/Critical, ~35–40% Deductive/Numerical, ~20–25% Spatial/Visual-Data:
- **Whose reasoning is correct? (8/paper, 20%, `THINK.DEDUCT.WHOSE_REASONING_CORRECT`):** Boxed rules in `stemPrefix` + two speakers; options `A <N1> only`, `B <N2> only`, `C Both`, `D Neither`. Tests contrapositive, "only if", "unless", necessary vs sufficient, scope shifts.
- **Identify concrete mistake (4/paper, 10%, `THINK.VERBAL.IDENTIFY_FLAW`):** *"Which one of the following sentences shows the mistake <Name> has made?"* Concrete counter-examples/overlooked cases in plain Year 6 English — zero Latin fallacy names.
- **Strengthen / Weaken (3–4/paper, ~3:1 weaken):** Everyday policy, experiment, or consumer arguments.
- **Must / cannot be true (2–4/paper):** `CONDITIONAL_CONTRAPOSITIVE` / `SET_SYLLOGISMS`. **Zero** parallel-reasoning or main-conclusion items.
- **Spatial & problem-solving staples:** Two-fold + cut paper unfolding (ideal for 2×2 SVG option tiles), 3D block/wire-cube views, complementary 3D pieces, row of standard dice (opposite faces sum to 7), unlabelled chart/floorplan matching from tables, minimum-question code deduction, tiered hire tables, network routes, relay team selection, truth-tellers/liars, seating/symbol chains.

## 4. Writing Blueprint (1 Task, 30 Min)
Rotate authentic NSW genres with clear audience/purpose, punchy title, 2–4 sentence scenario, and 3 open `bulletSuggestions` (plus optional `mandatoryOpeningLine`, visual prompt, or layout note):
- **2021 pattern:** Newspaper report (local paper) + visual stimulus illustration + 3 open bullets.
- **2022 pattern:** Diary entry (imaginative future setting) + `mandatoryOpeningLine` + 3 open bullets.
- **2023 pattern:** Competition email (judges) + 3 open bullets + practical layout note (e.g. no To/Subject headers needed).
- Also rotate advice sheets, persuasive letters, speeches, and narratives.
